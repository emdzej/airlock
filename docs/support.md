# Support

Something not working, or an idea for the apps or the appliance?

- **Read the guide first:** [User guide](/guide) and the
  [troubleshooting section of the Mac app page](/companion#troubleshooting).
- **Report a bug or ask a question:**
  [open an issue on GitHub](https://github.com/emdzej/airlock/issues).
  Mention the app and version (shown under **About Airlock**), your
  device and what you expected to happen.

## Common questions

**The app doesn't find my Airlock.**
The phone or Mac must be on the same network as the Pi. Guest and
corporate Wi-Fi often block Bonjour. Check that Airlock has
**Local Network** access in Settings › Privacy & Security › Local
Network.

**Do I need an Airlock to use the app?**
Yes. The app manages drives plugged into an Airlock: a Raspberry Pi 4
running the free Airlock image; see [Installation](/install).

**Where are drives in the Files app?**
Open Files › Browse. Each shared drive is listed under Locations as
soon as the app has seen it.
